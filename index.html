How the work actually goes

No mystery handoff, no subcontractor roulette. You know who is doing the work and where it stands.

01

Discovery

We work out what you actually need, which is often not what the initial brief says. Scope, budget, and constraints on the table before anything else.

02

Design and plan

Wireframes, mockups, or network diagrams — reviewed and approved by you before a single line of code or a single cable run.

03

Build and install

Execution with weekly progress updates. You see the work in progress rather than waiting for a reveal at the end.

04

Launch and hand over

Documentation, training, and credentials transferred to you. Ongoing support if you want it, no lock-in if you don't.
