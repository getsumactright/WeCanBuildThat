PRODUCT IMAGES
==============

Drop product photos in this folder, then put the filename in the `img` field
of the matching entry in shop.html.

    img: "wifi-router.jpg"     ->  images/products/wifi-router.jpg
    img: ""                    ->  placeholder card

A full https:// URL in `img` is also accepted and used as-is.

WHAT YOU MAY USE HERE
---------------------
  YES  Photos you took yourself of gear you own. Best option — real photos
       suit this page's "gear we actually use" premise better than catalog
       renders, and no other affiliate page has them.
  YES  Manufacturer press / media-kit images you have permission to use.
  NO   Images saved or hot-linked from Amazon product pages. Not permitted
       under the Associates Operating Agreement, regardless of how they were
       obtained. See IMAGES.md for the sanctioned API route.

FORMAT
------
  Aspect      4:3 works best (the card media area is 4/3). Other ratios are
              contained, not cropped, so nothing gets cut off.
  Size        ~1200x900 is plenty. Larger is wasted bandwidth.
  Type        .jpg for photos, .png only if you need transparency, .webp is
              fine and smaller if your host serves it.
  Background  Plain / white reads cleanest against the cream card.
  Naming      lowercase-with-hyphens.jpg, no spaces.
